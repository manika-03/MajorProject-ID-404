DESIGN DECISIONS

Accessibility is part of the structure.

This is a mobile design study. Sample links navigate between screen concepts; they do not sign in, record audio, or schedule native notifications.

  • 56 px primary controlsGenerous touch targets and clear spacing.
  • 16 px body textReadable defaults with larger-text preview.
  • Visible state + spoken cuesListening, speaking and stopped are explicit.
  • Screen-reader-first structureNamed controls, logical reading order and predictable navigation planned for the native app.
